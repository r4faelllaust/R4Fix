========================================
  CRAFT ANIMATOR — OPEN ERIC-STYLE RIGS
========================================

OPEN A RIG (easiest)
--------------------
1. Launch Craft Animator
2. Click the big blue "Open Rig" button
   (or drag your .fla onto the window)
3. Pick your cartman1235 / DeviantArt .fla file
4. Use the "Rig / Poses" panel on the right to
   scrub Eyes / Mouth frames (same idea as Animate)


WHERE TO GET THE APP ON YOUR DESKTOP
------------------------------------
https://github.com/r4faelllaust/R4Fix/actions

Download the artifact for your OS, unzip,
put the .exe / .AppImage / .app on your Desktop,
double-click.


TIPS FOR RIG AUTHORS
--------------------
• Prefer the .fla download (not only the tiny .xfl marker)
• If you saved Uncompressed XFL: zip the whole folder
  that contains DOMDocument.xml + LIBRARY/
• After import, click Library items to place extras on stage


KEYBOARD
--------
Space     play / pause
Arrows    nudge selection
[ ]       rotate
Ctrl+O    Open Rig
