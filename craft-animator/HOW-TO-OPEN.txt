========================================
  GET CRAFT ANIMATOR ON YOUR DESKTOP
========================================

Ignore "cd" and "npm" — you do not need them.


STEP 1 — Wait for the build (GitHub)
------------------------------------
After this update is on GitHub, open:

  https://github.com/r4faelllaust/R4Fix/actions

1. Click the latest "Build Craft Animator Desktop" run
2. Wait until it is green / finished
3. Scroll to Artifacts at the bottom
4. Download the one for your computer:

   • Windows → Craft-Animator-Windows
   • Mac     → Craft-Animator-Mac
   • Linux   → Craft-Animator-Linux


STEP 2 — Put it on YOUR Desktop
-------------------------------
1. Unzip the download
2. Drag the app to your Desktop:

   Windows:  Craft-Animator-Portable.exe
             (or the Setup .exe)

   Linux:    Craft-Animator-….AppImage
             (right-click → Allow running, then double-click)

   Mac:      open the folder and run Craft Animator


STEP 3 — Double-click
---------------------
A launcher opens (like a game client).
Press the big blue PLAY button.


IF YOU CANNOT FIND ACTIONS
--------------------------
Reply in chat: "I use Windows" or "I use Mac" or "I use Linux"
and ask: "send me the download link when the build finishes"


INSIDE THE APP
--------------
• Play on launcher = Maple puppet demo
• Eyes / Mouth buttons = face poses (like Animate rigs)
• Space = play / pause timeline
• File → Open = import .fla / .xfl puppets
